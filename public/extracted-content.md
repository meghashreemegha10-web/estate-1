Service FAQs
What is included in the standard package? Site preparation, foundation, RCC structure, block masonry, plastering, flooring, doors and windows, electrical and plumbing works, painting, standard waterproofing and final finishing. A full written specification document accompanies every quotation.
Can I upgrade materials or customize the design? Yes. Floor plans, elevations and finishes can be customized. Upgrades such as premium flooring, designer elevations or additional interior works are quoted in writing before execution.
How long does construction take? The standard timeline is approximately 12 months from commencement, subject to weather, approvals and design changes — the timeline is written into your agreement.
How are payments structured? Payments are stage-wise, linked to completed construction progress as defined in the agreement — you pay as your home rises.
Do you build on customer-owned plots? Yes. We construct on your own plot anywhere in our service areas, as well as on plots within our developments.
